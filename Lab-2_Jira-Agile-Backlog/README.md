## Objective

To gain hands-on experience with Agile project management using Jira by creating and organizing a product backlog, defining epics and user stories, assigning story points, planning a sprint, and tracking sprint progress.

## Work Completed

The assigned Telemedicine Slot Booking & Prescription Portal was managed as an Agile project using Jira.

The lab work included:

- Creating and configuring the Jira project
- Creating epics and user stories
- Organizing the product backlog
- Assigning story points
- Planning and starting a sprint
- Managing issues during the active sprint
- Tracking sprint progress
- Reviewing the sprint burndown chart
